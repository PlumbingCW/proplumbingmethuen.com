Local conditions

What plumbing looks like in Methuen

Methuen's tap water comes only from the Merrimack River, through one intake and one filtration plant that feed about 200 miles of water main. That is a single source for a city of this size, so a main break or a treatment issue can touch a lot of streets at once. If you lose pressure and your neighbours have too, the problem may be at the street, and the Methuen water emergency line at 978-983-8855 is the right first call.

If water is fine at the street but dead at your house, that is when you want an emergency plumber on the way.

The city's water report says homes built before 1940 may have lead service lines, and the water department is still replacing leftover lead goosenecks. The city publishes a service line inventory map, so older-home owners can look up their address before planning a repiping or water line job.

Frost matters too. The state building code table uses 48 inches for frost depth, so a shallow line or an outdoor spigot in an older house can freeze and split in a hard cold snap, and that is a classic after-hours call.

Part of the Methuen sewer system is combined, and the permit for the regional treatment plant lists outfalls to the Merrimack and the Spicket River for storm overflows. When a basement drain backs up in a downpour, we check your own line first, then tell you if the problem sits outside your property.

Low ground along the Bloody Brook and Jackson Street corridor sees flash flooding two to three times a year, according to the city's public works department. The Spicket River has flooded before too. If you live near there, a working sump pump is not optional. Our sump pump repair page explains what fails first.

Which problem is an emergency?

Not every plumbing problem needs a midnight visit, and we would rather tell you that than send a truck you do not need. Use this table as a guide. If you are unsure, call and describe what you see.

Emergency or can it wait until morning
What you seeCall nowCan wait a few hours
Pipe spraying or water pouring through a ceilingYes, shut the main and callNo

Gas smell is not a plumbing wait-and-see

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and the gas utility, Eversource, from outside. Do not flip switches. In Massachusetts gas work is licensed work, so it is not a job for a handyman.
