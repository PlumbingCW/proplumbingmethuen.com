Check before you hire

How to verify any plumber's Massachusetts licence

In Massachusetts only a master or journeyman plumber licensed by the Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters may do plumbing work. The same board licenses gas fitters, and gas piping is licensed work.

This applies to us and to every other company you call, so use it.

A four-step licence check

  • Ask the plumber for their licence type and number
  • Look the licence up on mass.gov
  • Confirm the licence is current and matches the name on their ID
  • Remember that permits are issued to licensed plumbers, not homeowners
No surprises

How pricing is explained

A plumber cannot price a leak by phone. They have to see it. So the process is: the plumber arrives, diagnoses the cause, explains the options and gives you the price before any work starts. You decide.

Prices shift with access, pipe material, parts, permit needs and the hour of the call. Our service pages name these factors without inventing numbers, so you can judge the quote when you hear it.

What you should get

  • The cause explained in plain words
  • A clear price before work starts
  • Options when more than one fix exists
  • A licensed plumber pulling any permit

What to walk away from

  • A price that changes after work begins
  • No licence number on request
  • Pressure to replace what can be repaired
  • A refusal to explain the work
