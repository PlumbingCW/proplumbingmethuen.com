How to tell what service you need

Start with the symptom, not the fix. Water where it should not be points to a leak or a burst line. A slow drain or gurgling toilet points to a clog. Lukewarm water points to the heater. A meter that keeps moving with everything off points to a hidden leak. The Massachusetts plumbing code (248 CMR) is the standard that governs the work, and which rules apply depends on the job.

What we do not do

We handle

  • Plumbing repairs, installs and replacements by licensed plumbers
  • Drain, sewer and water line work
  • Water heaters, boilers and sump pumps
  • Commercial plumbing calls

We do not handle

  • Gas leaks as a first call. Leave and call 911 and Eversource
  • Electrical repairs beyond turning off a breaker
  • Work that needs a permit without a licensed plumber pulling it
  • Promises about arrival times

In Massachusetts the local inspector issues plumbing permits to licensed plumbers, and minor repairs such as fixing a leaky faucet do not need one. Emergency work can start right away, but it must be inspected by the next working day. A licensed plumber handles that on jobs that need it.
