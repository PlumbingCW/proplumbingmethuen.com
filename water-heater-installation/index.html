Plan the unit

Sizing and fuel choice: start with how your house uses hot water

The right size depends on how many people shower, whether baths and a dishwasher run at the same time, and how fast the unit recovers. A tank that is too small runs out. One that is too large wastes heat all day.

Gas units

Venting and combustion air, in plain terms

A gas heater burns fuel, so it needs air to burn and a path for exhaust. The flue carries combustion gases outside and must slope and seal correctly. A tight utility room with no fresh air supply can cause poor burning and carbon monoxide.

Carbon monoxide detectors matter

Install or test a carbon monoxide detector near any gas appliance. If it alarms, leave the house and call 911. Do not wait to find out why.

Electric and heat pump units skip the flue but need space, air, and drainage. Manufacturer instructions govern every install, and they set minimum clearances that we follow.

Safety add-ons

Expansion tank, drain pan, and discharge piping

Heating water makes it expand. In a closed plumbing system with a backflow device or a pressure reducing valve, that pressure has nowhere to go, so an expansion tank absorbs it. Without one, the relief valve can drip and the tank takes extra stress.

Expansion tank

A small pressurized tank on the cold line that soaks up thermal expansion.

Drain pan

A shallow pan under the heater, piped to a safe drain, catches slow leaks before they reach the floor.

Relief valve discharge

The relief valve needs a discharge pipe that points down and ends where hot water cannot hurt anyone.

Shutoff valves

A cold supply valve above the heater means the next leak or repair is a quick shutoff, not a house-wide one.

If your old heater lacked any of these, expect them on the quote. Call (603) 815-9179 to walk through what your space needs.

Paperwork

Permits and who can do the work

In Massachusetts, only a licensed plumber may do plumbing work, and permits are issued to licensed plumbers. The Methuen Inspections Division issues plumbing permits. The 2022 fee schedule listed a 50 dollar line for a water heater, so check the current fees with the division before you budget.

You can verify any plumber's license on Mass.gov, where the Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters lists it. Be wary of anyone who offers to skip the permit. The permit is what puts an inspector's eyes on the gas, venting, and relief piping.

Fuel change

Switching from gas to electric or to a heat pump

Moving off gas can make sense if the flue is bad, the gas line is far away, or you want to cut combustion out of the house. It also means an electric circuit sized for the new unit and a plan for the old gas line, which a licensed gas fitter must cap properly.

A heat pump unit adds air space and a condensate drain to the checklist. Ask your electric utility and the state about heat pump incentives, because they change often. We can tell you what electrical work your panel needs. Existing units that have failed are covered under water heater replacement.
