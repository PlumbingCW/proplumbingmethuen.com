First steps

What to do before we arrive

For a burst pipe or active leak

  • Shut the main valve clockwise
  • Switch off breakers to wet areas if you can reach them dry
  • Move rugs and electronics out of the water
  • Open a low tap to drain the lines
  • Keep your phone close for our dispatcher's call

Water problems at the street, such as a broken main or a curb valve, belong to the town or water district. Your town page lists who to call. For a leak you cannot find, see leak detection.
