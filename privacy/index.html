Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you call Methuen Pro Plumbing or visit this website. We keep it simple because our service is call-only.

Last updated October 24, 2026
24/7 emergency line
(603) 815-9179 Call now

What we collect

When you call (603) 815-9179, we collect what you tell us: your name, phone number, service address, a description of the problem and any details you give the dispatcher. We use it to send a plumber and to explain the work and price.

Call recording

Ask the dispatcher about call recording when you call. If you do not want to be recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Website information

This website has no forms, no accounts and no payment pages. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, which can include your IP address, browser type, the pages you requested and the time of the request.

We use that information to keep the site running and to spot errors or abuse. Maps shown on the site are loaded from a third party and are subject to that provider's own policy.

How we use and share information

We use call details to dispatch a licensed plumber, to schedule your visit, to explain the price and to follow up on your job. We share the details needed for your job with the plumber assigned to it.

We may also share information when the law requires it or when needed to protect safety.

How long we keep information

We keep call details only as long as we reasonably need them for service, record keeping and legal reasons. Contact us at contact@proplumbingmethuen.com to ask about your information.

Your choices

  • Ask us what information we hold about your call
  • Ask us to correct details you gave us
  • Ask the dispatcher about call recording
  • Contact us with any privacy question

Contact and changes

Write to contact@proplumbingmethuen.com or call (603) 815-9179 with privacy questions. Our address is 251 Broadway, Methuen, MA 01844.

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Questions about your privacy?

Call the line and ask. A dispatcher will help.
